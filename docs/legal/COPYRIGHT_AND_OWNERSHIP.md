# Copyright & Manuscript Ownership Statement (Draft)

**You own your work.** Everything you write, plot, or otherwise create in Inkwell — manuscripts, characters,
world-building, storyboards, timelines — is yours. Inkwell claims no copyright interest in it.

## What we're licensed to do with it

Only what's needed to run the product for you: store it, back it up, sync it across your devices, and (only
when you explicitly ask) send a bounded excerpt to our AI provider or generate an export file. This license is
non-exclusive, revocable by deleting your content or account, and grants us no right to use your work for any
purpose beyond operating the service for you — see `docs/legal/AI_DATA_USE_DISCLOSURE.md` for the AI-specific
detail.

## AI-assisted content

Suggestions, brainstorming output, and findings the AI Assistant produces are shown to you clearly labeled as
AI-generated and separate from your own prose (per the product's design — see `docs/AI_ARCHITECTURE.md`). The
AI never inserts text into your manuscript automatically. [Whether/how AI-assisted suggestions you choose to
incorporate affect your own copyright claim in the resulting work is a live, evolving area of law that varies
by jurisdiction — a lawyer should address this directly rather than this draft attempting to.]

## Exported files

Files you export (DOCX, TXT, Markdown, backups) are yours outright, with no continuing claim by Inkwell.

## Third-party content you import

If you import content you don't own the rights to, that's between you and the actual rights holder — Inkwell
is a tool, not a rights clearinghouse, and takes no position on the copyright status of anything you choose to
import.
